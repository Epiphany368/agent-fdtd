## 1. Context and Problem Statement

Under Wave C of the Agent-Oriented FDTD Platform v4 architecture (`spec-agent-fdtd-platform-v4.md`), two fundamental physical grid issues historically blocked core platform convergence and production mesh speedup:

### 1.1 M1 Tooth Convergence Non-Monotonic Reversal (LEG 16 & LEG 17 Legacy)
- In LEG 16 (`docs/leg16-2026-10-19.md`), refinement of the 126 nm narrow tooth on the N8 guided fixture across three in-plane mesh rungs with fixed physical smoothing $t_{\text{phys}} = 20\text{ nm}$ ($[40/20, 20/10, 10/5\text{ nm}]$) exited with code **53** (`stopped-non-monotone`). Both transmission $T$ ($0.5648 \to 0.5744 \to 0.5737$) and effective index $n_{\text{eff}}$ ($2.0711 \to 2.0779 \to 2.0731$) reversed direction between $20/10$ and $10/5\text{ nm}$.
- In LEG 17 (`docs/leg17-2026-10-19.md`), an exhaustive seven-point grid/transition probe showed that holding the dimensionless smoothing resolution fixed ($s = t / \Delta x = 1.0$) produced strictly monotonic decrease:
  $$T: 0.645227 \to 0.574380 \to 0.569926 \quad (d_2 \le 0.5 d_1, p = 3.99)$$
  $$n_{\text{eff}}: 2.107171 \to 2.077889 \to 2.065529 \quad (d_2 \le 0.5 d_1, p = 1.24)$$
- **Physical Root Cause**: `smooth_geometry_epsilon` uses point-sampling on Yee coordinates rather than cell volume integration. Stepping $dx$ from $40 \to 20 \to 10\text{ nm}$ at fixed $t = 20\text{ nm}$ crossed the sub-Nyquist boundary from aliased ($s = 0.5$, edge weight $0.2165$) to resolved ($s = 1.0$, edge weight $1.4916$) to multi-cell continuum ($s = 2.0$). The reversal was an artifact of crossing the Nyquist sampling threshold, not a physical non-monotonicity of the grating tooth!

### 1.2 M4 Graded Mesh Modal Injection Accuracy Discrepancy (LEG 24 & LEG 36 Legacy)
- In LEG 24 (`docs/leg24-2026-10-19.md`), opt-in graded mesh yielded $28.29\%$ cell reduction, but exhibited relative errors of $\Delta T/T = -2.19\%$ and $\Delta R/R = +3.86\%$, exiting with code **57** (`verdict=partial`, failing the $10^{-3}$ threshold).
- In LEG 36 (`docs/leg36-2026-10-20.md`), longitudinal $x$-refinement removed $72.8\%$ of transmission error, but a persistent residual error of $0.5955\%$ in $T$ and $2.1932\%$ in $R$ remained.
- **Physical Root Cause**: The modal eigensolver operates on a uniform rectilinear grid. In uncollared graded mesh, immediate coarsening outside the launch rectangle altered numerical wave impedance and created transverse modal impedance mismatch with the evanescent tails in the cladding ($y > 0.22\,\mu\text{m}$) and substrate ($y < 0.0\,\mu\text{m}$), exciting spurious reflections and transmission phase errors.

## 2. Technical Implementation

### 2.1 Decoupled Scaled Smoothing Law (`src/leg47_m1_convergence.py`)
- Implemented `compute_decoupled_transition(case, cfg)`:
  $$t(\Delta x) = s \cdot \Delta x \quad (\text{with } s = 1.0)$$
  As the grid refines ($\Delta x \to 0$), the numerical transition layer smoothly contracts, recovering strictly monotonic convergence to the sharp binary boundary with observed order $p \ge 1.0$.
- Implemented `ladder_statistics(values, complete=False)`:
  - Tracks signed and absolute increments $d_k = X_{k+1} - X_k$.
  - Detects sign reversals ($d_k \cdot d_{k+1} \le 0$) and worsening error ($|d_{k+1}| > |d_k|$).
  - Computes observed convergence order $p = \log_2(|d_1| / |d_2|)$.
  - Performs Richardson extrapolation:
    $$X_\infty = X_{\text{fine}} + \frac{X_{\text{fine}} - X_{\text{mid}}}{2^p - 1}$$
  - Evaluates C1 pass criterion ($d_2 \le 0.5 d_1 + 10^{-12}$).
- Implemented `validate_config(cfg)`: Enforces positive refractive indices, positive grid steps, $0 < t \le w_{\text{tooth}}$, and at least 3 rungs.
- Supported seamless CPU and functional CUDA device execution with `select_device()`.

### 2.2 Uniform Modal Injection Collar Zone (`src/leg47_graded_collar.py`)
- Implemented `build_graded_collar_scene(cfg, case)`:
  - Establishes a **Uniform Injection Collar Zone** encompassing the launch waveguide, source plane ($x = -5.18\,\mu\text{m}$), outside monitor ($x = -5.34\,\mu\text{m}$), and near monitor ($x = -4.54\,\mu\text{m}$):
    - Longitudinal feed collar: $x \in [-6.10, -3.70]\,\mu\text{m}$ ($\Delta x \equiv 0.02\,\mu\text{m}$, 120 cells).
    - Transverse modal collar: $y \in [-1.75, +1.95]\,\mu\text{m}$ ($\Delta y \equiv 0.01\,\mu\text{m}$, 370 cells).
  - Exterior radiation and cladding zones grade smoothly with `mesh_grading = 1.25` ($\le 1.25$ verified on realised nodes) up to `coarse_limit = 0.065\,\mu\text{m}$ (> 20 ppw in silica).
- Timestep stability: Enforces `time_step_rule = 'realised_axis_minimum'`, ensuring $dt_{\text{graded}} \equiv dt_{\text{uniform}}$ (zero CFL reduction).
- Realised metrics: Implemented `check_collar_uniformity()`, `realised_metrics()`, and `calculate_cell_reduction()`.

### 2.3 Post-Flight Notarization Gates (`checks/post_notary.py`)
- **Check 8 (Monotonic Grid Convergence Gate)**:
  - Triggered by `convergence_ladder` or tag `monotonic_mesh_convergence`.
  - Verifies at least 3 completed rungs.
  - Rejects reversals or worsening errors with status `non_monotonic_mesh_ladder` (exit 66).
  - Computes observed order $p$ and Richardson limit $T_\infty$.
  - Awards certificate tags: `verified:monotonic_mesh_convergence` and `verified:richardson_extrapolated`.
- **Check 9 (Graded Mesh Collar Accuracy Gate)**:
  - Triggered by `graded_collar` or tag `graded_collar_accuracy`.
  - Evaluates $|\Delta T|/T < 10^{-3}$, $|\Delta R|/R < 10^{-3}$, and cell reduction $\ge 50\%$.
  - Rejects $|\Delta T|/T \ge 10^{-3}$ or $|\Delta R|/R \ge 10^{-3}$ with status `graded_accuracy_exceeded` (exit 66).
  - Rejects cell reduction $< 50\%$ with status `insufficient_cell_reduction` (exit 66).
  - Awards certificate tags: `verified:graded_collar_accuracy` and `verified:cell_reduction_50pct`.
